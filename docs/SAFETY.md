# Safety and responsible use

- **You are responsible for what is sent in your name.** Review the dry run and keep your profile accurate.
- **No deception.** Answers are true, even when the truth costs an application.
- **No bypassing.** It doesn't solve CAPTCHAs, avoid bot checks, type passwords, enter codes or create accounts.
- **Terms of service.** Some sites forbid automation, and some employers have rules about AI in applications. Check, and follow them.
- **Be considerate.** The filters exist so recruiters don't get spam. Apply where you fit and keep the volume reasonable.
- **Privacy.** There is no server and no telemetry. Your data stays on your computer, except what you send to employers, to your AI provider and through your mail connector.
- **The agent's Chrome profile holds your sign-ins.** Treat the `chrome-profile/` folder like a password, and don't share or back it up in the open.
